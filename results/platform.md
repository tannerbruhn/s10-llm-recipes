# The phone itself

| | Stock Android 12 | Mainline Linux (postmarketOS) |
|---|---|---|
| RAM available after boot | ~4.3 GB | **~7.2 GB** (+2.9 GB, +68%) |

- Wi-Fi (our driver port, 5 GHz): ~200 Mbit/s in 2-3 minute download tests, no stalls; ping ~3-9 ms to the router.
- 2.4 GHz on a crowded channel: 3-9 Mbit/s.
- Whole-phone transfers to the phone currently top out around 5-15 MB/s.
